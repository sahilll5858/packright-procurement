The problem

Malpani Group buys corrugated cartons and packaging materials every season. Quotations arrive in completely different formats — one vendor quotes INR all-inclusive, another quotes USD with freight on top, a third quotes ex-works without GST. Comparing them manually takes hours, hides costs, and is error-prone.

What this assistant does

· Standardises every quote into one table (landed-cost INR incl. GST + freight, lead-time days, burst-strength grade 0–10).
· Compares vendors on weighted criteria you control (Quality, Cost, Lead Time, Reliability).
· Enforces policy — below minimum strength grade = flagged and excluded, whatever the price.
· Recommends one vendor with transparent reasoning, then you hold the final word (review / override).
· Never moves money — output is always a draft approval record for the Finance team.
